# Glyph candidates, parked test (v0.3.2, about 3 min)

Goal: find a real note-like symbol the car draws. The car swaps ♪ ♫ for a dot, so a glyph that is **not** drawn shows as a **dot**.
Parked only. One action per step. Photos are fine here.

1. Start the car.
2. Wait for "Now Steering is on" on the phone.
3. Open Now Steering → **Diagnostics**.
4. Under "Car test screens", tap **Glyph candidates**.
5. Look at **line 1** (big line): `♩ ♬ ♭ ♯ ♠`. Note which show as a real symbol (not a dot, box or blank). 📷
6. Look at **line 2** (disc icon): `♣ ♥ ♦ ☼ ♀`. Note the same. 📷
7. Look at **line 3** (person icon): `♂ « » ○ ◊`. Note the same. 📷
8. Tap **♪ glyph**. Note: dots again? (the control)
9. Tap **Back to live**.
10. Under "Song on the nav screen", check that **▶ (play, default)** is selected.
