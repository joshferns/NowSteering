# Session 2 script (~5 min, parked)

**Before (at home):** install the latest APK over the old one. In NS Probe tap **Clear**, then:
**untick** Request audio focus · tick **Forward…** · mode **a) metadata only** · Title box: type `PROBE ON` ·
Mirror **off**. You need a second phone (or a person) to call you in steps 13 and 15.
Before each step, type its number in *Add note* and tap **Add note**.

1. Connect to the car. Note: is the car **still** showing the stuck call from last time?
2. In YouTube Music, play a song and drag it to **~60 s before its end**.
3. In NS Probe, tap **START session**, then **Send Title**. Note: car shows `PROBE ON`? YTM audible?
4. *(Audio A/B)* Listen 15 s with the probe ON. Note: muddled? (yes/no)
5. Tap **STOP session**. Listen 15 s. Note: muddled?
6. Tap **START session**. Listen 15 s. Note: muddled?
7. *(Song ends naturally)* Let the song end. 10 s into the next song, note: car shows `PROBE ON` or the YTM song?
8. *(Re-grab)* If the car shows the YTM song: tap **Re-grab**. Note: back to `PROBE ON`?
9. *(Skip via steering, forwarded)* Press steering **Next** once. Note: did YTM skip? What does the car show?
10. If the car shows the YTM song: **Re-grab**. Note result.
11. *(Skip on phone)* In YTM, tap next. Note: what does the car show?
12. If the car shows the YTM song: **Re-grab**. Note result.
13. *(Call, probe OFF)* Tap **STOP session**. Have someone call you; answer, talk 10 s, hang up **on the phone**.
    Note: does the car screen go back to normal within 10 s? Do the steering buttons work?
14. Tap **START session** (YTM still playing). Send Title again.
15. *(Call, probe ON)* Same call again. Note the same two things.
16. Add note `end`, tap **Share text**, send it to me with your notes.

If the car gets stuck on the call again: note at which step, then try turning phone Bluetooth off and on and say whether that clears it.
