# Phase 1 in-car test (~10 min + a short drive)

**Expect:** turn arrows show as `•` on this first drive. Maps only gives the arrow as a picture; this drive
logs each picture so I can label them for the next build. Distance, road and ETA should already be right.

**At home, once:** install NowSteering.apk, do the 5 permission buttons and **Choose car** (see README).
Open **Diagnostics** → **Clear**. Pick a known route (e.g. home → office, ~5 min of it is enough).
You need someone who can phone you in step 11.

## Parked (one action per step; note what you see)
1. Start the car and wait for Bluetooth to connect. Note: does the phone show "Now Steering is on" within ~15 s?
2. Look at the car screen. Note: clock + `-- km/h` (or `0 km/h`), date, `Now Steering`? 📷
3. Start YouTube Music playing. Note: music audible? What does the car show now?
4. Start Google Maps navigation to your known destination. Note: within ~5 s, does the car show `• <distance>` / road / `ETA …`? 📷
5. Press steering **Next** once. Note: did the song change?
6. Press steering **Previous** once. Note: did the song change back / restart?
7. Press head-unit **Pause** once. Note: did the music pause?
8. Press head-unit **Pause** again. Note: did the music resume?
9. Double-tap steering **Next**. Note: car shows the clock screen?
10. Double-tap steering **Next** again. Note: back to the nav screen?
11. Have someone phone you. Note: does the car show its normal incoming-call screen?
12. Answer on the phone, talk ~10 s, hang up on the phone.
13. Wait 10 s. Note: is the car back to the nav screen, with no stuck call display?
14. Press steering **Next** once. Note: do the buttons still work?

## Drive (glance only, never touch the phone; a passenger can note for you)
15. Drive the route. At each turn, note: was the distance counting down? Was the road name right? Was anything stale, wrong or stuck?
16. On a straight stretch, double-tap **Next**. Note: does the speed roughly match the speedometer?
17. Double-tap **Next** to go back to nav.
18. Arrive (or end navigation once parked). Note: does the car switch to the clock screen?

## After
19. Turn the car off. Note: does "Now Steering is on" disappear within ~10 s?
20. Diagnostics → **Share file** (the log will be big) and send it to me with your notes and photos.
