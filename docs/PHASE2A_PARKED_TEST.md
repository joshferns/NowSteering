# Phase 2a parked test (v0.3.0, about 10 min; 0.3.1 changes step 25 only)

Rules: parked only. One action per step. Photos are fine here, never while driving. No voice dictation.

**At home**
1. Install NowSteering.apk. Settings → Apps → Now Steering: version **0.3.0**.
2. Open Now Steering → **Diagnostics** → **Clear**.

**In the car**
3. Start the car.
4. Wait for "Now Steering is on" on the phone.
5. Open Now Steering → **Diagnostics**.

**The ♪ glyph and widths** (Diagnostics → "Car test screens")
6. Tap **♪ glyph**. Look at the car. Note: ♪ shows as a note / box / dot / blank? 📷
7. Tap **Song widths**. Note: last visible letter on line 2; how many M/W show on line 3. 📷
8. Tap **Line 2 text**. Note: is "pm" visible on line 2? Is "19:42" complete on line 3? 📷
9. Tap **Bars**. Note: are all blocks visible on both lines? 📷
10. Tap **Mirror preview**. Note: does it look right? 📷
11. Tap **Back to live**.
12. In "Song on the nav screen" choose **♪** if step 6 showed notes; if not, choose **• (dot)**.

**"Show on car" button**
13. Open the regular YouTube app. Play any video.
14. Look at the car. Note: does it show the video's title?
15. Pull down the notification shade. Tap **Show on car**.
16. Look at the car. Note: Now Steering clock screen?

**"Keep Now Steering on screen"** (regular YouTube still playing)
17. Open Now Steering. Tick **Keep Now Steering on screen**.
18. Wait 5 s. Look at the car. Note: `▶ video title` and `m:ss left` bar? 📷
19. In YouTube, start a different video.
20. Wait 5 s. Look at the car. Note: still Now Steering?
21. Pause the video on the phone.
22. Look at the car. Note: back to the clock screen?
23. Play the video again.
24. Press steering **Next** once. Note: what happened (video skipped / nothing)?
25. *(Removed in 0.3.1: triple-tap Next no longer exists.)*
26. Untick **Keep Now Steering on screen**.
