# Phase 1 test 4 (~3 min parked + your normal commute)

**At home:** install the new NowSteering.apk. Diagnostics → **Clear**.

## Parked: does Power saving hurt? (one action per step)
1. Start the car; wait for "Now Steering is on".
2. Turn **Power saving ON** (quick settings).
3. Lock the phone. Watch the car 15 s. Note: speed `0 km/h` or `-- km/h`?
4. Unlock. Turn **Power saving OFF** again (or leave it on if step 3 showed `0 km/h`).

## Arrival
5. Drive a navigated trip as usual (phone locked).
6. On arrival, note: does `● Arrived` stay on until you drive off, or until you double-tap Next?
